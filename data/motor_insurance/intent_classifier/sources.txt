DATASET CONSTRUCTION METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Step 1 — Source Selection
  Public datasets were selected based on:
  (a) Open license (CC BY 4.0 or public domain) for academic use
  (b) Semantic overlap with Indian motor insurance domain
  (c) Availability of intent labels to map to our 4-class taxonomy

Step 2 — Intent Mapping
  External intents were mapped to our 4-class taxonomy as follows:

  External Intent Category           → InsureReg Label
  --------------------------------------------------
  "premium calculation", "IDV"       → calculation
  "regulation", "law", "mandatory"   → regulatory
  "coverage", "policy terms"         → coverage
  "claim", "complaint", "document"   → claim_process

Step 3 — Adaptation
  - No verbatim copying of any copyrighted text.
  - Paraphrasing retained semantic meaning and intent class.
  - Indian context added (IRDAI, MVA 1988, INR, lakh) where applicable.

Step 4 — Labelling Verification
  - Each row independently reviewed for label consistency.
  - Ambiguous rows were assigned to the most prominent intent.

Step 5 — Class Balance Check
  calculation:   25 rows (25.8%)
  regulatory:    18 rows (18.5%)
  coverage:      29 rows (29.9%)
  claim_process: 25 rows (25.8%)
  — All classes adequately represented for a capstone demonstration.
