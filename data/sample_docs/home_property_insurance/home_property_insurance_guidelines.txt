CHAPTER 1: TYPES OF HOME & PROPERTY INSURANCE
------------------------------------------------

1.1 Standard Fire and Special Perils (SFSP) Policy
The SFSP policy is the base policy for all property insurance in India, as prescribed by
IRDAI/IRA (Insurance Regulatory Authority). It is governed by the India Market Tariff.

Covered Perils:
- Fire
- Lightning
- Explosion/implosion
- Aircraft damage (impact of aircraft or articles dropped from aircraft)
- Riot, strike, and malicious damage (RSMD)
- Storm, tempest, flood, and inundation
- Impact damage (by rail/road vehicles or animals)
- Subsidence and landslide
- Bursting or overflowing of water tanks or pipes
- Missile testing operations
- Leakage from automatic sprinkler installations
- Bush fire

1.2 Householder's Insurance Policy (Package Policy)
A comprehensive package policy that covers:
- Building structure (walls, fixtures, fittings)
- Contents (furniture, appliances, personal belongings)
- Burglary and theft
- Personal accident for the policyholder
- Third-party liability within the premises
- Loss of rent if property is uninhabitable

1.3 Structure-Only Policy
Covers only the physical building structure, including:
- Walls, roof, foundation
- Fixed installations (plumbing, electrical wiring, fitted kitchens)
- Boundary walls, gates, and compound
- Does NOT cover contents, furniture, or movable items

1.4 Contents-Only Policy
Covers only the movable contents inside the property:
- Furniture and furnishings
- Electrical and electronic appliances
- Clothing and personal effects
- Jewellery and valuables (subject to specified sub-limits)
- Kitchen equipment and utensils

CHAPTER 2: SUM INSURED AND VALUATION
----------------------------------------

2.1 Valuation Basis
As per IRDAI guidelines, the sum insured can be determined by:

a) Reinstatement Value: Cost of rebuilding/replacing to the same condition as new
   - Most recommended for building structure
   - Premium is higher but provides full replacement cost

b) Market Value: Current market value after depreciation
   - Lower premium but inadequate coverage due to depreciation deduction at claim time

c) Agreed Value: Sum insured agreed upon by insurer and insured based on valuation report
   - Requires independent valuation by a certified surveyor
   - Most suitable for commercial properties

2.2 Under-Insurance and Average Clause
- If the sum insured is less than the actual value of the property, the Average Clause applies
- Claim payout = (Sum Insured / Actual Value) × Loss Amount
- Example: Property worth INR 50 lakh insured for INR 25 lakh
  If loss = INR 10 lakh → Claim = (25/50) × 10 = INR 5 lakh only
- To avoid this, insured must accurately declare the full value

CHAPTER 3: NATURAL CALAMITY COVERAGE
---------------------------------------

3.1 Earthquake Coverage
- NOT automatically covered under SFSP — must be added as an optional extension
- Earthquake Fire and Shock coverage: Available as an add-on
- Premium: Additional percentage of sum insured based on seismic zone
- Seismic Zone classification:
  Zone V (Very High Risk): North-East India, J&K, Uttarakhand — Highest premium
  Zone IV (High Risk): Delhi, parts of UP, Bihar — Higher premium
  Zone III (Moderate Risk): Mumbai, Chennai — Moderate premium
  Zone II (Low Risk): Most of Peninsular India — Lowest premium

3.2 Flood and Waterlogging
- Storm, tempest, flood, and inundation are COVERED under SFSP
- Waterlogging due to overflow of drains or water bodies: Covered
- Seepage/dampness: Generally EXCLUDED
- Damage to property stored in basement during flood: May have sub-limits

3.3 Cyclone and Windstorm
- Covered under SFSP as part of "Storm and Tempest"
- Includes damage from wind, rain accompanying the storm, and flying debris
- Exclusion: Damage caused solely by rain without storm/wind is NOT covered

CHAPTER 4: BURGLARY AND THEFT COVERAGE
-----------------------------------------

4.1 Burglary Insurance (Housebreaking)
Coverage includes:
- Loss of contents due to burglary or housebreaking
- Damage to the premises caused during the burglary
- "Housebreaking" = entry by use of force and violence to the building

Requirements for valid claim:
- Visible signs of forced entry (broken locks, windows, doors)
- FIR filed with police within 24 hours of discovery
- Inventory of stolen items with approximate values
- Photographs of damaged entry points

4.2 Theft vs. Burglary
- Burglary: Involves forcible entry — COVERED
- Theft: Loss without evidence of forced entry — Generally NOT covered
- Mysterious disappearance: NOT covered
- Loss by employees or domestic servants: NOT covered under standard policy
  (can be covered under Fidelity Guarantee policy)

4.3 Jewellery and Valuables
- Sub-limit applies: Typically 20-30% of contents sum insured
- Must be specifically declared with individual values
- Valuation certificate from a certified jeweller is required
- Items stored in a bank locker: NOT covered (separate policy required)

CHAPTER 5: CLAIM PROCESS FOR HOME INSURANCE
----------------------------------------------

5.1 Claim Intimation
- Inform the insurer within 24 hours of the incident
- For theft/burglary: File FIR with police immediately
- For natural calamities: Take photographs of damage before any repairs
- Do not discard damaged items until surveyor inspection

5.2 Required Documents
- Duly filled claim form
- FIR copy (for theft/burglary/malicious damage)
- Photographs of damaged property/stolen items area
- List of damaged/stolen items with purchase receipts (if available)
- Repair estimates from contractors
- Weather reports (for natural calamity claims)
- Municipal/fire department reports (for fire claims)
- Policy document copy
- KYC documents and bank details

5.3 Surveyor Assessment
- Insurer appoints a licensed surveyor for claims above INR 50,000
- Surveyor must submit report within 30 days (IRDAI mandate)
- For claims below INR 50,000: Simplified settlement without surveyor
- Policyholder can dispute the survey report and request reinspection

5.4 Claim Settlement Timeline (IRDAI mandate)
- Claim must be settled within 30 days of receipt of all documents
- If surveyor is appointed: Within 30 days of surveyor's report
- Maximum time: 6 months from date of claim intimation
- Interest payable by insurer for delayed settlement beyond 30 days

CHAPTER 6: EXCLUSIONS
------------------------

6.1 Standard Exclusions
- War, invasion, foreign enemy action
- Nuclear risks and radiation
- Pollution or contamination (unless caused by covered peril)
- Wear and tear, gradual deterioration
- Vermin, insects, mildew, or damp
- Electrical/mechanical breakdown (unless caused by covered peril)
- Loss or damage to property undergoing construction, alteration, or repair
- Wilful negligence by the insured
- Property used for commercial/industrial purposes (residential policy)
- Damage caused by pets owned by the insured

6.2 Terrorism Cover
- Terrorism is EXCLUDED from standard SFSP policy
- Available as a separate add-on: Terrorism Risk Insurance cover
- Pool arrangement through General Insurance Corporation (GIC) of India
- Premium: Additional percentage based on location and sum insured
