CHAPTER 1: HEALTH INSURANCE PRODUCT TYPES
-------------------------------------------

1.1 Individual Mediclaim Policy
Provides hospitalization coverage for the insured individual. Covers inpatient treatment
expenses including room rent, doctor fees, surgery charges, and medicines.

Key Parameters:
- Minimum sum insured: INR 1,00,000
- Maximum sum insured: Up to INR 1,00,00,000 (1 Crore)
- Policy period: 1 year (renewable annually), 2-year, or 3-year options
- Entry age: 91 days to 65 years (lifelong renewability after first policy)
- No claim bonus: 5%-50% increase in sum insured for each claim-free year

1.2 Family Floater Policy
Single policy covering the entire family with a shared sum insured.
Family definition: Self, spouse, dependent children (up to 25 years), and parents.
- Advantage: Cost-effective compared to individual policies for each member
- Risk: One large claim can exhaust the coverage for the entire family

1.3 Critical Illness Policy
Lump sum benefit paid on diagnosis of specified critical illnesses.
Covered illnesses (as per IRDAI standardization):
- Cancer of specified severity
- Myocardial infarction (first heart attack)
- Open chest CABG (Coronary Artery Bypass Graft)
- Kidney failure requiring dialysis
- Major organ transplant
- Stroke resulting in permanent symptoms
- Multiple sclerosis
- And others as per policy terms

1.4 Top-Up and Super Top-Up Policies
- Top-Up: Activates when a single claim exceeds the deductible threshold
- Super Top-Up: Activates when aggregate claims in a year exceed the deductible
- Deductible range: INR 3,00,000 to INR 10,00,000 (common for corporate employees)

CHAPTER 2: PRE-EXISTING DISEASE (PED) NORMS
----------------------------------------------

2.1 IRDAI Standardized Waiting Periods
As per IRDAI Health Insurance Regulations 2024:

- Pre-existing diseases: 36 months (3 years) waiting period from first policy inception
  (Previously 48 months — reduced to 36 months as per latest IRDAI directive)
- Specific diseases/procedures: 24 months waiting period
  (e.g., cataract, hernia, joint replacement, benign prostatic hypertrophy, etc.)
- Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start (except accidents)
  (No claims payable in first 30 days except for injuries from accidents)

2.2 Pre-Existing Disease Declaration
- Proposer must declare all known medical conditions in the proposal form
- Non-disclosure or misrepresentation can lead to claim rejection
- After 8 years of continuous coverage, the insurer cannot reject a claim on grounds
  of non-disclosure of pre-existing conditions (Section 45 read with IRDAI guidelines)

CHAPTER 3: CASHLESS CLAIM PROCESS
------------------------------------

3.1 Network Hospital Cashless Facility
Process flow:
Step 1: Policyholder visits a network hospital listed by the insurer/TPA
Step 2: For planned hospitalization — submit pre-authorization form 48 hours in advance
Step 3: Hospital submits cashless request to TPA/Insurer
Step 4: TPA/Insurer verifies coverage and approves/rejects within:
  - Pre-authorization: 1 hour from receipt of complete documents
  - Enhancement: 2 hours from receipt of enhancement request
  - Final bill settlement: Within 3 hours of discharge (IRDAI mandate)
Step 5: Insurer settles the bill directly with the hospital
Step 6: Policyholder pays only non-covered expenses (if any)

3.2 Required Documents for Cashless Claims
- Health insurance ID card / policy number
- Photo ID proof (Aadhaar/PAN/Passport)
- Pre-authorization form filled by the treating doctor
- Doctor's prescription and treatment plan
- Diagnostic reports (as prescribed)

CHAPTER 4: REIMBURSEMENT CLAIM PROCESS
-----------------------------------------

4.1 When Reimbursement Applies
- Treatment at non-network hospitals
- Cashless request denied or hospital not empanelled
- Emergency treatment where cashless was not possible

4.2 Claim Submission Timeline
- Claim intimation: Within 24 hours of hospitalization (for planned admissions: 48 hours before)
- Document submission: Within 15 days of discharge from hospital
- Late submission: Up to 30 days with valid reason; beyond 30 days at insurer's discretion

4.3 Required Documents for Reimbursement
- Duly filled claim form
- Original hospital bills and receipts (itemized)
- Discharge summary from hospital
- Investigation/diagnostic reports
- Pharmacy bills with prescriptions
- Doctor consultation records
- KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, bank details)
- FIR (in case of accident claims)

CHAPTER 5: POLICY PORTABILITY
-------------------------------

5.1 IRDAI Portability Guidelines (Circular No. IRDAI/HLT/REG/CIR/296/11/2024)
- Policyholders can port their health insurance from one insurer to another
- Porting must be applied at least 45 days before the renewal date
- The new insurer must give credit for the waiting periods already served
- No break in coverage allowed — porting is only during renewal

5.2 Portability Benefits
- Pre-existing disease waiting period credit: Time already served is carried forward
- No claim bonus: Accrued NCB is transferred (in terms of sum insured increase)
- Continuous coverage recognition: No fresh waiting period for ported policies

5.3 Portability Process
Step 1: Policyholder applies to new insurer at least 45 days before renewal
Step 2: New insurer requests portability data from old insurer via IRDAI portal
Step 3: Old insurer must provide data within 7 days
Step 4: New insurer underwrites and issues offer within 15 days
Step 5: Policyholder accepts and pays premium to new insurer

CHAPTER 6: EXCLUSIONS (STANDARD AS PER IRDAI)
-------------------------------------------------

6.1 General Exclusions Applicable to All Health Policies
- Cosmetic or aesthetic treatment (unless required due to accident)
- Dental treatment (unless requiring hospitalization)
- Congenital external diseases or defects
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Treatment related to substance abuse or alcoholism
- War, nuclear contamination, or terrorism-related injuries
- Experimental or unproven treatments
- Maternity expenses (unless specifically covered by rider/floater)
- Obesity treatment/surgery (unless medically necessary with BMI > 40)
- Refractive error correction (LASIK) — unless sum insured > INR 5 lakh

6.2 Room Rent Sub-Limits
- Standard policies may have room rent capped at 1% or 2% of sum insured per day
- If actual room charges exceed the sub-limit, all associated costs are proportionally reduced
- IRDAI encourages insurers to offer policies without room rent sub-limits

CHAPTER 7: AYUSH COVERAGE
---------------------------

7.1 IRDAI Mandate for Alternative Medicine Coverage
As per IRDAI directive, all health insurance policies must offer coverage for:
- Ayurveda
- Yoga and Naturopathy
- Unani
- Siddha
- Homeopathy

Coverage rules:
- Treatment must be taken in a government-recognized AYUSH hospital
- Hospital must be registered under the Clinical Establishments Act
- Sum insured for AYUSH is same as for allopathic treatment
- All standard claim documentation requirements apply
